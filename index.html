Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before and after your Hijama therapy session.

Hijama (wet cupping) is an ancient therapeutic practice that creates localized vacuum suction on specific body points, followed by shallow micro-incisions to facilitate blood circulation and assist the body in eliminating stagnant fluid and muscular tension.
Your session begins with a brief health consultation. You will relax comfortably while single-use sterile cups are applied for suction. Small micro-incisions are then made gently, which most clients describe as barely noticeable, followed by thorough sanitization.
We recommend refraining from heavy meals 2 to 3 hours prior to your visit. Shower before coming in, wear comfortable loose-fitting clothing, and stay well hydrated.
A typical Hijama treatment takes approximately 45 to 60 minutes, including intake, suction setup, wet cupping, and post-treatment cleansing.
Most healthy adults can receive Hijama safely. We do not recommend wet cupping for individuals on heavy blood-thinning medications, severe anemia, active skin infections, or pregnant women without medical clearance.
Keep the cupped areas clean and moisturized with natural black seed or olive oil provided. Avoid strenuous exercise, hot showers/saunas, and cold drafts for 24 hours. Drink plenty of water.
